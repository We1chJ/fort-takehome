I am motivated by the level of rigor, flexibility, and ownership I can bring to a mission-driven environment. At this stage of my life, I care about two things: to create and to challenge. I like creating unique products and delivering tangible solutions that make a direct impact in people’s hands, rather than purely digital abstractions. That’s why I went on stage twice at the Tech Roast Show in Boston, where my team built a live system using Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses to stream vision in real time. Under intense live-audience pressure, we reverse-engineered the hardware, stitched together mobile interfaces, and deployed the cloud infrastructure and backup systems required to keep the stream running flawlessly. We won the show and were featured on Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/reel/DNvb6_e4gHF/). Executing under high pressure taught me the value of extreme end-to-end ownership, rapid prototyping, customer-centric thinking, and a strong bias for action.

With this mindset, I want to be at a place where I can do things that no one else has done before, and build at the frontline where I have to navigate to the right path among endless possibilities. As an engineer, my goal is to leave an impact by putting tangible products into the hands of users that they can hold onto even after I move on from the project. Deeply aware of my limited bandwidth individually, I am motivated to create products because I can scale my impact horizontally. As a result, being able to interact with users directly in the development process would fuel me even further.

Therefore, I am drawn to apply to Fort because it aligns with my experience in wearables, health metrics, and physical devices. I have had corporate internships in the past, and honestly, I did not like them because they were too big and too slow. The mission and scale felt too distant and broad, and I felt completely detached from them. I was only working on side projects and felt like I was capable of much more. At Fort, I hope to take true ownership of the product development cycle in a fast-paced environment. I want to be on the frontline, talking directly to users, learning from them, and applying both my engineering and people skills to tackle their issues. I hope to have the freedom to openly explore designs, work on ambitious tasks, and apply my software and embedded systems skills to see what kind of future I can build for the users.
